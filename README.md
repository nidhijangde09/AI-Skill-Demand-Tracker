# AI Skill Demand Tracker 📊

An interactive dashboard that tracks demand for AI, data, and analytics skills across job postings — highlighting the most in-demand tools, the AI/ML vs. data-analytics split, hiring trends by experience level, and top locations.



## 🔍 Overview

This dashboard analyzes **5,000 job postings** across **10 key skills** to surface hiring trends in the AI and data space. It's designed to help job seekers, recruiters, and analysts quickly spot which skills are trending and where the demand is concentrated.

## ✨ Features

- **Summary cards** — Total jobs, total skills tracked, top overall skill, and top AI-specific skill at a glance
- **Top in-demand AI and data skills** — Horizontal bar chart ranking skills like Python, SQL, Machine Learning, Power BI, Excel, NLP, AWS, Generative AI, Deep Learning, and Tableau by job mentions
- **AI/ML vs. Data Analytics split** — Donut chart comparing demand across the two skill categories
- **Jobs by experience level** — Trend line showing job volume across Entry, Mid, Senior, and Lead roles
- **Top skills by location** — Bar chart comparing demand across cities including Singapore, Remote, New York, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, London, and Pune
